Zon Institute

An institutional parent framework for a range of activities, connections, events, and business units.

Institutional role

Zon Institute acts as a parent root under which different institutional areas can operate. Rather than representing a single program or activity, the Institute provides a broader organizational framework.

Its structure is designed to accommodate multiple areas of activity, including Connections Management, events, business units, and future categories.
